    Backup and restore

    Your records are stored in this browser on this device. Download a spreadsheet backup regularly, and use it to move your records to another device or browser.

    The backup has one sheet for your cars and one for the maintenance log, and opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. VIN photos are not included.

    AutoLog stores everything in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
